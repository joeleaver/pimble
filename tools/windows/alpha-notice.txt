Pimble is an alpha, and it sends diagnostics

While Pimble is being tested, every copy sends its developers what they need to
see what went wrong:

- warnings and errors, and how syncing and sharing go;
- your stores' names and where they are on disk;
- your computer's kind: Windows version, processor, memory.

It never sends anything you wrote in a note, your notes' titles, your email
address, or any password or key.

Records carry a random install number, and your account's number (not your
email) when you are signed in, so your devices and both ends of a share can be
read together.

Turn it off at any time in Settings > Diagnostics. Turned off, nothing more is
sent and what waits on this computer is deleted.
